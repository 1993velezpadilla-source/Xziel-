# Community-port / reference asset lane

Last reviewed: 2026-09-20

This lane is intentionally separated from the future commercial Xziel asset pool.

## Purpose

Use this document to record:
- visual references
- animation timing observations
- map-layout references
- compatibility experiments
- names/locations of community-made or extracted assets that need a rights check

It is NOT a redistribution whitelist.

## Proprietary game extractions

Assets extracted from Call of Duty or other commercial games remain proprietary unless the rights holder has separately licensed them for reuse.

Rules for this public repository:
1. Do not commit proprietary ripped meshes, textures, animations, audio, maps or cinematics.
2. Do not label a rip as "safe" merely because the community port is free.
3. Store only research notes, hashes/identifiers where lawful/necessary, replacement requirements and links to legitimate source releases.
4. When a community asset is genuinely original and released under CC0/CC BY/GPL-compatible terms, record its exact license and it may graduate into the appropriate safe bucket.
5. Prefer recreating the feel with legal replacement assets and original code.

## Replacement workflow

For a proprietary reference:
- identify what makes it recognizable (silhouette, timing, stride cadence, recoil profile, lighting rhythm, particle behavior);
- reproduce those gameplay qualities using original or licensed assets;
- avoid copying protected textures/models/audio;
- document the replacement's source and license.

## Commercial firewall

Nothing in this lane may be copied into the future commercial Xziel game until it passes the commercial-safe license policy.

This protects the long-term project from accidental contamination while still allowing research on the community-port experience.
