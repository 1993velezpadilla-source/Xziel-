# BO6 Launch/Season Maps + BO7 Launch — Exact Progression Research

Updated: 2026-09-20

Purpose: preserve the remaining dependency-accurate quest structures that were still summarized elsewhere in this research corpus: Black Ops 6's Liberty Falls, Terminus, Citadelle des Morts and The Tomb, plus Black Ops 7's launch map Ashes of the Damned.

These notes are original normalized research. Randomized values must always be read from the player's current match.

# BLACK OPS 6

## Liberty Falls — Bye-Bye, Dark Aether

### Required order

1. **Open the route to Hilltop Church.**
   - Pack-a-Punch is already usable once the church is reached; there is no separate power switch.

2. **Start the Main Quest at the S.D.G. Generator.**
   - Interact with the side panel.
   - Let Dr. Panos finish the first dialogue.
   - Interact a second time.
   - Do not continue until the dialogue confirms the signal link; one interaction alone does not properly start the quest.

3. **Build the Thrustodyne M23 / Jet Gun.**
   - Water Pressure Gauge:
     - obtain the valve;
     - install/use it at the Liberty Lanes pressure panel;
     - hold the area until pressure reaches 100%.
   - Handbrake:
     - kill the red-shirted Groundskeeper in the Cemetery;
     - take his key;
     - open his tool shed;
     - take the Handbrake.
   - Electrical Wires:
     - use a Mangler cannon charge-shot to open the Radio House;
     - search the debris/trash piles for the wires.
   - Craft the Jet Gun at the Motor Lodge hidden second-floor workbench.

4. **Vacuum the three L.T.G. parts with the Jet Gun.**
   - Hilltop Church balcony above the S.D.G.
   - Washington Avenue open-window route near Speed Cola.
   - Olly's Comics ceiling.
   - Build the L.T.G. on the Alamo rooftop bench.

5. **Stage the first Aether Canister.**
   - Remove a canister from the church device.
   - Carry it to one of the two field traps.
   - Carrying forces walking, so stage it before starting the timed HVT sequence.

6. **Place the L.T.G. under the first mini storm.**
   - Survive the 60-second defense.
   - Zombies touching the portal drain its integrity.
   - If it fails, retry on a later round.

7. **Capture HVT essence #1.**
   - An elite spawns: super-Mangler or Abomination depending on storm.
   - Weaken it without killing it.
   - Wait for the purple-smoke cue.
   - Lure it onto the staged field trap.
   - Activate the trap.
   - Kill the HVT while stunned on the trap.
   - Its essence enters the canister.

8. **Return the charged canister to the church within about 1:30.**
   - The player still cannot sprint while carrying it.
   - After the first successful capture, take the Strauss Counter.

9. **Calibrate the three Projectors.**
   - Use the Strauss Counter at each projector.
   - Set the projector to the inverse color:
     - Counter green → projector red.
     - Counter red → projector green.
     - Counter yellow → projector yellow.
   - The live readings matter; do not use a fixed chart for a previous match.

10. **Repeat the HVT capture at storm #2.**
    - Stage the second canister.
    - Defend the second L.T.G.
    - Weaken the second elite until purple smoke.
    - Kill it on the trap.
    - Return the canister inside the carry timer.
    - Killing the HVT incorrectly is recoverable; retry later rather than restarting the run.

11. **Prepare for the S.D.G. shutdown.**
    - Upgrade weapon rarity.
    - Pack-a-Punch II+.
    - Tier 2–3 armor.
    - Perks.
    - Mutant Injection / scorestreak.
    - Jet Gun ready.
    - In co-op, the full team votes to begin.

12. **Survive the three-wave shutdown.**
    - Maintain the church position.
    - Use the Jet Gun for crowd control.
    - The final wave culminates in elite pressure including an elite Mangler and Abomination.
    - Clearing the last wave intentionally downs the whole team.
    - Do not interpret the scripted down as a wipe; wait for the ending/completion sequence.

### Design observations

- This is BO6's most accessible Main Quest because the dependency graph is short and failures are usually retryable.
- The two HVT loops teach the rule once, then ask the player to repeat it under stronger pressure.
- The canister's movement restriction makes route preparation matter.
- Directed Mode can expose the same underlying quest with stronger guidance rather than separate content.

## Terminus

### Required order

1. **Activate all three AMP Generators.**
   - Generator 1 at spawn/Prison.
   - Generator 2 in Living Quarters.
   - Generator 3 in Bio Lab.
   - Each is a defense event.

2. **Raise Pack-a-Punch.**
   - Dive into the Bio Lab pool.
   - Activate the lift/platform mechanism.

3. **Activate the three laptops.**
   - X near the Sea Tower/docks route.
   - Y near storage/Stamin-Up route.
   - Z in Communications.
   - If a laptop will not register, use X first.

4. **Open the Research Office with Dead Wire.**
   - Put Dead Wire on a weapon.
   - Ride the Pack-a-Punch inclined lift.
   - Shoot the first electrical box through the moving-lift window.
   - Follow the electric chain through the lift shaft and Living Quarters boxes.
   - Shoot the final exterior box.
   - Enter the newly opened Research Office.

5. **Recover the EMF Fob.**
   - Kill the trapped zombie inside the office.
   - Take the dropped fob.

6. **Recover the Multiphasic Resonator.**
   - Open the Sea Tower briefcase.
   - Take the resonator.
   - Take the battery if present in the run.
   - Install the resonator on the Research Office computer.

7. **Solve or buy the Beamsmasher keypad code.**
   - The legitimate route derives X/Y/Z from the live whiteboard math.
   - Alternatively pay Peck 5,000 Essence and read the answer from his monitor.
   - Wait for the next-round targeting completion.
   - Take the resonator back before leaving.

8. **Complete the three island orb rituals.**
   - Travel by Tactical Raft.
   - Place the resonator beneath the blue orb.
   - Kill the glowing blue-marked zombies over/near the resonator and feed the essence.
   - Pick the resonator back up.
   - Repeat with the green orb.
   - Repeat with the purple orb.
   - The resonator converts into the infused injector.

9. **Build the DRI-11 Beamsmasher.**
   - Return to the Research Office bench.
   - Craft it.

10. **Free the hard drive from the correct Tentacle Trap.**
    - The correct trap is identified by the Project Janus keycard lying under it.
    - Activate the trap.
    - Fire the Beamsmasher alt-fire into the tentacles.
    - Recover the hard drive.
    - Deliver it to Peck at the Guard Station.

11. **Read Nathan's three-digit tank code.**
    - Digit 1: hour hand from the Interrogation clock.
    - Digit 2: number on the pinned playing card in the Mess Hall.
    - Digit 3: Days Since Last Injury sign in Engineering.
    - Enter them in that fixed source order.

12. **Open Nathan's chamber.**
    - In co-op, use the required simultaneous levers.
    - Survive the lockdown.
    - Kill Nathan, the empowered Amalgam.

13. **Recover the gold keycard.**
    - Dive into the Bio Lab water after the cutscene.
    - Take the keycard.
    - Activate the green-text Communications computer.

14. **Recover the Node Connectors from the Shipwreck.**
    - Raft to the crashed ship.
    - Enter the underwater/workshop route.
    - Take the connectors.
    - The hatch locks.
    - Survive the roughly two-minute lockdown.
    - Carrying a connector disables sprint.

15. **Repair the randomized broken pipes.**
    - Check Crab Island shore.
    - Sea Tower dock pipe.
    - Sea Caves / Speed Cola route.
    - Install connectors only where the live match shows a gap.

16. **Obtain the hacking device from Peck.**

17. **Hack all three buoys.**
    - First successful hack starts a two-minute timer.
    - Raft between all three.
    - Complete each held interaction before the timer expires.

18. **Return immediately to Bio Lab and defuse the three bombs.**
    - The bomb timer is about five minutes.
    - Each defuse requires a long held interaction.
    - This is a genuine run-ending fail state: if the timer expires, the facility detonates.
    - Aether Shroud/Monkey Bombs are ideal solo protection.

19. **Prepare for Patient 13.**
    - After all three bombs are defused, the hard timer is gone.
    - Take as many rounds as needed before entering the marked boss door.

20. **Patient 13 phase 1.**
    - Attack the red spore-like weak points on the shoulders/sides.
    - Clear tentacles when it submerges.

21. **Patient 13 phase 2.**
    - Damage the mouth and glowing arm-tentacle weak points.
    - Avoid the lethal central-water danger zone.

22. **Patient 13 phase 3.**
    - Damage the eyes.
    - When the blue-smoke wipe attack charges, get behind cover.
    - Avoid the tongue/suction line.
    - Finish the boss.

### Design observations

- The quest uses the island geography as an actual logistics problem: boats, routes and timed hacks matter.
- The 5-minute bomb stage is a rare true hard fail after many otherwise retryable steps.
- The game gives unlimited preparation time immediately after the dangerous timed sequence instead of chaining pressure unfairly.

## Citadelle des Morts

### Required order

1. **Breach the castle.**
   - Reach the Courtyard.
   - Rotate the cannon toward the blocked gate.
   - Fire it.

2. **Raise Pack-a-Punch.**
   - Reach the Undercroft/oubliette.
   - Shoot the three locks.
   - Kill the spawned Doppelghast.
   - Take the Blood Ethereum Crystal.
   - Insert it into the portal/device.

3. **Meet Gabriel Krafft.**
   - Find his dungeon door.
   - Let the quest dialogue finish.
   - Open the viewing flap/interact.

4. **Collect the four torn book pages.**
   - Search the Sitting Rooms.
   - Four of several possible page spawn points are active in the match.

5. **From round 10 onward, collect four knight stamps.**
   - Kill helmeted knight zombies.
   - Give one stamp to each knight statue.
   - Claim the four basic Bastard Swords.

6. **Upgrade the Lion sword / Light.**
   - Align the crystal-laser chain through the castle.
   - Take the Paladin's Brooch.
   - Place it in its ritual circle and fill with souls.
   - Use the Lion sword on the lion door.
   - Melee glowing parasites to charge the sword.
   - Deposit four charged symbols into the door.
   - Enter the sanctuary.
   - Place the sword.
   - Shoot the prompted matching symbols.
   - Reclaim the upgraded sword.
   - Use it to open the Light Incantation chest.

7. **Upgrade the Dragon sword / Fire.**
   - Place the basic sword in the Dragon statue.
   - Collect flame from each of the three braziers.
   - Sprint the burning charge back to the statue before health reaches zero.
   - After the third deposit, claim the upgraded sword.
   - Light the three ritual bonfires.
   - Kill the resulting Doppelghast for Ra's Ankh.
   - Place the Ankh, fill the soul ritual and open the Fire Incantation chest.

8. **Upgrade the Stag sword / Lightning.**
   - Collect all three Lightning Rods:
     - fixed rod near Deadshot.
     - Dead Wire electrical-box route.
     - random zombie drop.
   - Place all three in the rampart vase.
   - Kill the storm's special glowing zombies with the Stag sword.
   - Melee the vase when the sword is electrically charged.
   - Repeat until all rods are charged.
   - Claim the upgraded sword.
   - Recover and charge the Pegasus Horseshoe.
   - Complete its soul ritual.
   - Open the Lightning Incantation chest.

9. **Upgrade the Raven sword / Void.**
   - Search the Alchemical Lab for the one active Antiquity/bone relic.
   - Identify the relic's zodiac sign and corresponding element.
   - Insert the Antiquity and Raven sword into the Tavern Cellar ring device.
   - Align the element and zodiac glyphs.
   - Herd the spawned purple orbs through all three portal positions.
   - Reclaim the upgraded Raven sword.
   - Shoot the raven during the cave-slide route.
   - Shoot it again outside.
   - Recover Raven's Talon.
   - Complete the soul ritual.
   - Open the Dark Incantation chest.

10. **Open the hidden symbol wall.**
    - Acquire Melee Macchiato.
    - Melee the cracked Undercroft wall.
    - Read the six live-match vase/symbol relationships.
    - Shoot/input the symbols in their correct numerical order.

11. **Restore the ritual book.**
    - Place the four page fragments.
    - Read the book's trap order.

12. **Complete the four Point of Power traps in book order.**
    - Match the live symbols.
    - Clear each trap's required kills.
    - Preserve the order shown by the current match.

13. **Perform the four knight invocations.**
    - Read the animal order.
    - At each knight, use the correct matching Incantation.
    - Inspect/use the corresponding elemental sword.

14. **Take the Mystic Orb.**

15. **Complete the four elemental Orb trials.**
    - Fire.
    - Lightning.
    - Light.
    - Shadow.
    - Each trial uses the matching sword/incantation language established earlier.

16. **Claim the Guardian Key.**

17. **Prepare at the Town Square fountain / boss gate.**
    - Legendary, PaP III conventional bullet weapon recommended.
    - The swords are not a replacement for armor-breaking gun damage.
    - Co-op uses a team vote.

18. **Fight The Guardian.**
    - Break the stone armor.
    - Attack exposed critical areas.
    - Survive statue/add phases.
    - Use the previously learned elemental tools for control while relying on conventional weapons for efficient boss damage.

### Design observations

- Four elemental weapon branches share a common grammar but have different skill tests: beam alignment, dangerous carrying, enemy-charge timing and symbolic deduction.
- The quest then recombines all four branches into one common ritual chain.
- Many symbol inputs are per-match; the environment remains the authoritative source.

## The Tomb

### Required order

1. **Reach the Subterranean Temple.**
2. **Place the Citadelle amulet at the Door to Nowhere.**
3. **Open the Dark Aether Nexus.**
4. **Access Pack-a-Punch in the Nexus.**

5. **Wait until round 8 for the guaranteed Shock Mimic.**
   - Kill it.
   - Take the Monocle.

6. **Solve the Hunter mural.**
   - Manipulate the burning-crystal light source until the mural is illuminated.
   - Read the live symbol arrangement.
   - Shoot the Roman-numeral symbols in numerical order.
   - Complete the resulting mini-lockdown.
   - Take the Staff headpiece.

7. **Solve the Bull mural.**
   - Repeat the light-placement logic.
   - Shoot the eight Roman-numeral symbols in numerical order.
   - Complete its mini-lockdown.
   - Take the Staff piece.
   - Wrong inputs are forgiving; resume from the last correct number.

8. **Build the Staff of Ice in the Dark Aether Nexus.**
   - Install all three parts.
   - Defend the machine for about 60 seconds.
   - Purple-smoke enemies specifically damage the Staff build.
   - If the build fails, advance a round and retry.

9. **Freeze three burning crystals within roughly ten seconds.**
   - Reposition crystals beforehand by destroying them and waiting for respawn.
   - Freeze three with the Staff quickly enough.
   - Failure is retryable.

10. **Read the three floating Nexus symbols.**
    - Shoot three symbol-bearing rocks with the Staff.
    - Record the three symbols.
    - One exit portal seals.

11. **Reproduce the symbols at the sealed portal.**
    - Leave by another portal.
    - Go to the sealed portal's map-side door.
    - Shoot the three matching symbols.
    - Wrong answer causes the high-platform fall/failure and a next-round retry.

12. **Escort the purple orb.**
    - If the symbol answer was correct, start the orb on the high platform.
    - Stay near it.
    - Feed kills near it so it continues moving.
    - In co-op, the team must remain with the escort.
    - If it escapes/fails, retry next round.
    - At completion, use the Staff at the central machine to upgrade it into Ull's Arrow.

13. **Complete the four creature trials.**
    - Use the upgraded Staff's alternate end to take energy from a Nexus podium.
    - Carry the energy to the matching map statue.
    - Complete that trial.
    - Recover the energy.
    - Bring it back through the required return route.
    - Energy has its own health and can be destroyed by enemy attacks.

14. **Vermin trial.**
    - Survive the heavy Vermin flood.

15. **Parasite trial.**
    - Kill marked Parasites.
    - Carry their purple drops to the statue before time runs out.

16. **Doppelghast trial.**
    - Survive mass Doppelghasts plus floor spikes/lasers.

17. **Amalgam trial.**
    - Break the elite's bubble by killing the tethered armored zombie.
    - Then damage the Amalgam.

18. **Extend the bridge.**
    - All four completed trials fire beams toward the boss door.
    - Prepare fully.
    - Interact with the Sentinel Artifact/team vote.

19. **Sentinel Artifact encounter.**
    - Ordinary bullet weapons damage the Artifact; explosive damage and the Staff do not.
    - Orange artifact phases are laser-survival phases.
    - Transparent purple/blue phases are damage windows.
    - Later artifacts are carried by empowered special enemies.
    - Kill the carrier, then immediately damage the dropped Artifact.
    - Use vertical position to dodge sweeping lasers.

20. **Final timed Artifact burn.**
    - The final Artifact moves away from the central formation.
    - Destroy it before the wipe timer expires.
    - Failure wipes the team/end of quest.
    - Interact with the completed Artifact to finish the ending.

### Design observations

- This quest explicitly separates **crowd-control Wonder Weapon** from **boss-damage conventional weapon**.
- Failure policies vary intelligently: murals and escort retry, while the final Artifact burn is a deliberate hard fail.
- The four creature trials reuse one transport framework but vary their combat rule.

# BLACK OPS 7

## Ashes of the Damned — Dust to Dust

### Stage 1 — vehicle, power and Pack-a-Punch

1. **Recover T.E.D.D.'s head from Janus Towers Plaza.**
2. **Install the head on Ol' Tessie.**
3. **Drive to Blackwater Lake / Vandorn Farm / Ashwood.**
4. **Clear the spore infestations from all three local generators.**
5. **Turn on all three generators.**
6. **At Ashwood, fit Pack-a-Punch to the rear of Tessie.**
   - Pack-a-Punch is now mobile with the vehicle.

### Parallel objective — collect six Serum ingredients

7. Collect all six in any order while doing the rest of the quest:
   - Human Bones.
   - Ravager Eyes.
   - Horde Husk Chunks.
   - Fungus / Widow's Lantern.
   - Mysterious Limb.
   - Powder of the Forgotten.

Each ingredient uses a different acquisition verb: trap kill, weapon/equipment interaction, timed biological growth, vehicle kill or Wisp/Gauntlet manipulation.

### Stage 2 — Klaus, the canister and the Gauntlet

8. **From roughly round 8+, kill Uber Klaus at Janus Plaza.**
   - Break shoulder armor.
   - Destroy the exposed core.
   - Take the Stabilizer Chip.

9. **Wake normal Klaus in the Ashwood Sheriff's Office.**
   - Install the chip.
   - Bounce several Stun Grenades into the correct jail interaction.
   - The player who wakes Klaus controls him.

10. **Take Klaus to Zarya Support Systems.**
    - Command him to break the wall panel.
    - Stand alone in the retina-scan circle.
    - Face the scanner beam while the scan progresses.
    - Defend the scanning player.
    - Take the Aether Canister.

11. **Complete the three-generator timed carry.**
    - Pre-position Tessie before picking up the canister.
    - Carrying disables sprint and jump-pad use.
    - Charge at Ashwood.
    - Reach Blackwater before Refined Energy Decay expires.
    - Charge there.
    - Reach Vandorn Farm.
    - Charge there.
    - Failure restarts the whole three-generator run rather than only the missed leg.

12. **Insert the canister into the Farm-cellar cube.**

13. **Read the three random barn-roof glyphs from the jump-pad flight.**
    - Record right / middle / left in the live match.

14. **Set the three cube faces to those glyphs.**
    - Leave the eye face untouched until submission.
    - Submit at the eye face.

15. **Claim the Necrofluid Gauntlet.**

16. **Complete the Gauntlet tutorial lockdown.**
    - The player is rooted at the floor sigil.
    - Fire a barb into each green target.
    - Recall the barbs through the targets.
    - Use detonation/recall correctly until all six are destroyed.

### Stage 3 — Serum and fog keys

17. **Open Yuri's Lab at Zarya.**

18. **Decode the live pigpen cipher.**
    - Three words appear top-to-bottom.
    - Match each word to the corresponding Serum ingredient on the lab table.
    - Interact with the three items in order.
    - A wrong sequence creates black smoke and is retried next round.

19. **Add blood and survive the roughly 90-second lab lockdown.**
    - The donor is health-limited with no normal regeneration during the event.
    - Kill zombies inside the room so souls feed the canister.
    - Going down fails the step and it is retried next round.

20. **Collect the three glowing fog keys.**
    - Use the Gauntlet to pull each free.
    - Each key applies a different debuff:
      - one removes health regeneration;
      - one forces melee-only;
      - one removes sprint.
    - Transport them to the Ashwood pyramid.

21. **Insert all three keys.**
    - The chains break.
    - Trigger the mid-quest cinematic.

22. **Take the DG-2 turret component.**
23. **Install the turret on Tessie.**

### Stage 4 — stop time and complete the four POI quests

24. **Shoot the ethereal clock tower with Tessie's DG-2 turret.**
    - This stops time for the current round.

25. **Use the Gauntlet on a clock face to turn the world-state purple.**
    - Three usable purple windows per round.
    - The clock must be stopped again on later rounds.
    - Every POI trial below must be performed in this altered state.

26. **Vandorn Farm ritual.**
    - Read the farmhouse clock.
    - Stand on the corresponding floor Roman-numeral position.
    - Wait for the father figure's red-eye cue.
    - Shoot it with the Gauntlet.
    - Collect the four ritual props.
    - Place them.
    - Reactivate all four quickly.
    - Defend all four objects for about 60 seconds.

27. **Exit 115 frozen-lightning ritual.**
    - Wait for the diner lightning state.
    - Pre-position Tessie and Klaus.
    - While purple, shoot the service-station clock.
    - Complete the three resurrection/escort interactions:
      - Waitress.
      - Engineer.
      - Trucker.
    - Lead each revived spirit/zombie into its matching lightning.
    - Souls persist between attempts, making this recoverable over rounds.

28. **Zarya rocket ritual.**
    - While purple, stop both spinning satellite dishes with Gauntlet barbs.
    - Read the six-letter pigpen word from the monitors.
    - Convert each letter using the map's A=0 indexing rule.
    - Lock the six current-match numeric monitors from left to right.
    - Successful input launches the rocket.
    - Failure rerandomizes the word and requires another satellite setup.

29. **Blackwater Lake film-reel ritual.**
    - After the other three POIs, interact with the dock wisp.
    - While purple, shoot the projector.
    - Memorize the randomized four-location film order.
    - Shoot the four reels in that exact order.
    - Wrong order jumpscares/fails and is retried next round.
    - Summon Klaus.
    - Direct him to the upstairs wisp-covered lockbox so he retrieves/carries the required object toward the docks.

### Stage 5 — Wisp drive and boss

30. **Prepare at Blackwater Lake.**
    - This is the point of no return.
    - Maximize Pack-a-Punch/rarity.
    - Carry armor/Kazimirs.
    - Keep salvage for Tessie repair.
    - Ready GobbleGums/Field Upgrade.

31. **Interact with Klaus and complete the team vote.**

32. **Wait for the wisps to distribute across the map.**

33. **Drive the Wisp lap in Tessie.**
    - Collect exactly three souls in each fog zone.
    - Do not leave a fog zone before all three are collected.
    - Leaving early fails the run segment and requires a later revote.
    - Use the turret gunner to clear road mines.
    - Drive the full route through the major POIs.

34. **Collect the final soul.**
    - The team is teleported directly into the Veytharion arena.

35. **Veytharion vehicle-combat loop.**
    - Drive until Tessie's boost is charged.
    - Boost up the central ramp.
    - Ram Veytharion.
    - Fire Tessie's beam.
    - A turret player shoots the exposed mouth weak points.
    - On foot, shoot only unarmored critical areas.

36. **Handle immunity phases.**
    - Veytharion becomes invulnerable at major health thresholds.
    - Wait for the blue beam.
    - Reach it with Tessie before the boss.
    - Drive through it to infuse the vehicle.
    - Boost-ram the boss to break immunity.
    - Damage the critical mouth window.

37. **Survive the late purple laser.**
    - Take cover behind pillars or interrupt with a properly timed ram.

38. **Maintain Tessie.**
    - Repair after destruction using salvage.
    - Heal the vehicle by driving through green spore pods.
    - Save the arena's persistent Max Ammo/Armor pickups for need.

39. **Kill Veytharion and complete Dust to Dust.**

### Design observations

- Ol' Tessie is simultaneously traversal, Pack-a-Punch carrier, weapon platform, timed-transport solution, survival resource and boss mechanic.
- The four POI quests deliberately use four different genres: ritual defense, resurrection escort, cipher/code, and memory sequence.
- The purple world-state turns ordinary landmarks into quest spaces without needing four separate submaps.
- The final boss pays off the vehicle skills learned across the entire map.

# Cross-map design rules exposed by these five maps

1. **Retry policy should be local.**
   - Liberty HVT fail → retry later.
   - Tomb mural fail → resume/retry.
   - Ashes lab fail → retry next round.
   - Terminus bomb fail → true match-ending failure.
   - Final boss wipe → deliberate hard fail.

2. **Current-match information must be enough.**
   - Liberty projector colors.
   - Terminus math/tank/pipe states.
   - Citadelle symbols/zodiac/trap order.
   - Tomb floating symbols.
   - Ashes roof glyphs/pigpen/reel order.

3. **Signature tools should have several jobs.**
   - Jet Gun: combat + L.T.G. collection.
   - Beamsmasher: combat + tentacle interaction.
   - Elemental swords: combat + rituals/incantations.
   - Ice Staff: combat + environment + upgrade + trials.
   - Necrofluid Gauntlet: combat + object pull + time-state puzzle + ritual activation.

4. **The boss should pay off the map.**
   - Patient 13 uses Terminus' water/island arena language.
   - Guardian uses the castle's elemental preparation and armor-breaking.
   - Sentinel Artifact uses the Nexus' vertical hazard language.
   - Veytharion turns Tessie into the centerpiece instead of abandoning the vehicle at the end.

5. **Preparation gates should be explicit.**
   Every irreversible finale should have an obvious interaction/team vote and enough time beforehand to shop, repair and choose equipment.
