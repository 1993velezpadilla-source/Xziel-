# Black Ops 7 Cursed Relic Catalog

Updated: 2026-09-20

Purpose: preserve Black Ops 7's **Cursed Mode / Relic** layer as a reusable reference for Xziel challenge design.

This is a design-research catalog, not a copied walkthrough. Exact unlock routes are normalized from current guides and official mode descriptions. One relic remains intentionally marked **UNSOLVED** because the community does not yet have a verified trigger.

## System overview

Cursed is a stripped-back Zombies ruleset:

- pistol start / no normal loadout weapon;
- limited HUD;
- no Main Quest objective guidance;
- Black Ops III-style point economy;
- Pack-a-Punch costs are doubled;
- players equip previously unlocked Relics to add disadvantages.

Relics are **challenge modifiers, not buffs**. Their combined weight raises the run's Cursed Tier:

- Grim = 1/3 tier;
- Sinister = 2/3 tier;
- Wicked = 1 full tier;
- Tier I unlocks access to Golden Armor;
- Tier II unlocks Ultra weapon rarity;
- Tier III unlocks Pack-a-Punch IV.

Relic unlock pattern:

1. Complete that map's Main Quest at least once.
2. Enter Cursed on the correct map/tier.
3. Perform a hidden side objective.
4. A Relic Portal appears.
5. Enter the trial.
6. The player's equipped relics are disabled inside the trial.
7. Complete the trial's special rules.
8. The relic becomes permanently selectable for future Cursed runs.

Important persistence rule:
**The discovery/setup and Relic Trial must be completed in the same sitting.** Save-and-quit or leaving mid-chain can reset or break specific unlocks.

As of 2026-09-20:
- 30 relics are named/cataloged across five maps.
- 29 have verified community unlock routes.
- Totenreich's Wrestler's Belt is the only unresolved trigger.
- Rex Infernus launched with no relics of its own; relics unlocked on the other maps can still be equipped there.

# Ashes of the Damned — 9 Relics

Ashes has 3 Grim, 3 Sinister and 3 Wicked relics. Its three Grims alone add up to Tier I, making Ashes the cleanest starting point for a fresh Cursed progression track.

## Grim

### Lawyer's Pen

Effect:
- Mimic/Shock-Mimic props infiltrate ordinary map loot/prop interactions.

Gate:
- Round 20+.
- Any Cursed tier.
- Ashes Main Quest previously completed.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Use Napalm Burst or Molotov/fire damage to light the three hidden red candles.
2. Confirm the Mister Peeks cue.
3. Enter the Vandorn Farm portal.

Trial:
- Four waves.
- Farm-localized.
- Shock Mimic pressure.

Xziel mechanic class:
**environmental scavenger → ambush modifier → localized combat trial**

### Dragon Wings

Effect:
- Normal power-up drops are disabled.

Gate:
- Round 20+.
- Any tier.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Use the Vandorn Farm / Janus Towers jump pad.
2. During one flight, shoot the three purple targets.
3. Enter the Farm portal.

Trial:
- Four waves.
- Touching power-ups damages the player rather than helping.

Xziel mechanic class:
**movement skill-shot → inverted reward vocabulary**

### Teddy Bear

Effect:
- The delay between rounds is reduced by roughly 75%.

Gate:
- Nine collectibles can be progressed early.
- Final/tenth target requires round 20+.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Obtain the Necrofluid Gauntlet.
2. Use Aether Shroud.
3. Find/strike the Mister Peeks doll set.
4. Finish the final doll at the round gate.
5. Enter the Farm portal.

Trial:
- Four waves.
- Every gunshot costs Essence.

Xziel mechanic class:
**collectible hunt → economy-as-ammunition trial**

Xziel mechanic class:
**run-long collection discipline → gun-game modifier**

### Focusing Stone

Gate:
- Round 40+.
- Tier I+.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Finish a Zursa with the required melee condition.
2. Obtain the required Legendary T.E.D.D. reward.
3. Complete the bottle/memory interaction.
4. Enter the Flame Trench portal.

Trial:
- Five waves.
- The player's existing Essence balance is held/removed for the duration and returned on success.

Xziel mechanic class:
**multi-system prerequisite → temporary economy confiscation**

# Paradox Junction — 3 Relics

One relic per tier, naturally forming a round-20 / round-40 / round-60 progression ladder.

## Grim — Rocket

Effect:
- Scorestreaks are unavailable.

Gate:
- Round 20+.
- Any tier.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Manipulate/pet the required friendly hound interactions across the two timelines.
2. Use the D.A.W.G. / current quest tool setup.
3. Enter the portal.

Trial:
- Each enemy type can only be killed with its matching elemental weakness/ammo mod.

Xziel mechanic class:
**cross-world creature interaction → elemental-counter test**

## Sinister — Summoning Key

Effect:
- Zombies explode on death and can damage nearby players.

Gate:
- Can be prepared early.
- Final trigger round 40+.
- Tier I+.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Recover the required objects across Nuketown states.
2. Perform the deliberate-down interaction at the correct gravestone/location.
3. Enter the portal.

Trial:
- Five timed waves.
- Roughly 90 seconds per wave.

Xziel mechanic class:
**intentional player-down mechanic → timed survival**

## Wicked — Mangler Helmet

Effect:
- Arsenal machine is removed from the map.

Gate:
- Round 60+.
- Tier II+.

Unlock skeleton:
1. Use the Mister Peeks Field Upgrade with Mystery Box interactions across both timelines.
2. Preserve prerequisites that conflict with other relics.
3. Complete the round-60 knife/Mimic interaction.
4. Enter the portal.

Trial:
- Six waves.
- No armor plates.
- Reduced maximum/usable health.

Xziel mechanic class:
**run-long secret collection → stripped-defense endgame trial**

Important incompatibilities:
- Mangler Helmet removes the Arsenal needed by Rocket's elemental trial.
- Astra's Seed removes the Mystery Box needed for Mangler Helmet.
- Dying Wish can interfere with Summoning Key's deliberate-down trigger.

Design lesson:
Relics are not merely independent toggles. **Modifiers can interact or conflict**, forcing planned challenge runs.

- Three waves.
- Trap kills only.

Xziel mechanic class:
**observation hunt → environment-kills-only combat**

## Grim — Wrestler's Belt — UNSOLVED

Known/documented effect:
- Wall-buy weapons randomize each round.

Verified unlock trigger:
- **Unknown as of 2026-09-20.**

Current evidence:
- Treyarch previously stated the relic was not bugged.
- Current guide indexes still mark its activation method unsolved.
- Datamined material suggests a trial where weapons may be removed/taken at the end of waves, but that is **not a verified legitimate unlock route**.

Xziel lesson:
Leave room for real community mysteries.
Do not expose every secret through data/UI immediately, and never rewrite an unsolved mystery as solved merely because datamined behavior exists.

Effect:

Gate:
- Round 60.
- Tier II+.

1. Reach round 60 without losing a single Assault Wave flag.

Design lesson:
**Mutually exclusive challenge conditions can create intentional replay routes** without needing branching story content.

# Why this system is unusually valuable for Xziel

## 1. Difficulty is player-authored

Instead of selecting only Easy/Normal/Hard, the player chooses *how* the run becomes harder:

- economy restriction;
- healing restriction;
- mobility pressure;
- ammo restriction;
- perk instability;
- enemy mutation;
- damage-type restriction;
- infrastructure removal.

That produces more expressive challenge runs.

## 2. The disadvantage buys access to new power ceilings

A crucial BO7 idea is:

**more handicaps → higher Relic Tier → stronger systems become purchasable**

This is different from a simple difficulty multiplier.

An Xziel version could use:
- Tier I → regenerating armor relic.
- Tier II → mythic weapon rarity.
- Tier III → fourth weapon-transformation tier.
- Tier IV, if ever used → cosmetic/prestige only, not raw mandatory power.

## 3. Unlock quest and equipped effect are separate ideas

A relic's unlock challenge does not need to mirror its permanent modifier exactly.

Example design space:
- unlock via parkour;
- modifier affects ammo.
- unlock via murder mystery;
- modifier changes elite spawns.
- unlock via no-damage survival;
- modifier removes bleedout.

This dramatically expands authored variety.

## 4. Cross-relic conflicts create planned runs

Some BO7 relics directly conflict with other unlock routes.

Xziel should support metadata such as:

- `conflictsWith`
- `requiresModifierTier`
- `requiresQuestState`
- `invalidatesUnlock`
- `recommendedRunGroup`

The menu should warn about **known hard conflicts** once the player has discovered both conditions, without revealing undiscovered secrets.

## 5. One-session discovery has real tension

The side quest + portal + trial must be completed in one sitting.

For Xziel:
- preserve one-session mastery for optional prestige challenges;
- do **not** use it for ordinary story progression;
- record partial discovery in the journal even if mechanical progress resets.

## 6. Unknown secrets should remain first-class state

The Wrestler's Belt is a good reminder that a shipped game can contain an unsolved community secret months later.

Xziel research tooling should distinguish:

- Known.
- Suspected.
- Datamined but unverified.
- Developer-hinted.
- Solved.
- Patched.
- Removed.

Do not collapse these into one boolean.

# Proposed Xziel implementation model

```
CursedModifierDefinition
{
    id
    displayName
    severity: Grim | Sinister | Wicked
    tierWeight
    persistentUnlocked
    effectRules[]
    sourceMap
    unlockQuestId
    trialQuestId
    prerequisites[]
    conflicts[]
    knownState: Unknown | Suspected | Verified
}

RelicTrialDefinition
{
    roundGate
    cursedTierGate
    mapStateGate
    allowedDamageSources
    disabledSystems
    timeLimit
    waveRules[]
    hvtRules[]
    failurePolicy
    coOpScaling
}
```

Runtime requirements:

- modifiers compose deterministically;
- conflict resolution happens before match start;
- trials temporarily suppress equipped modifiers when required;
- server owns trial state;
- no save/load exploit should duplicate unlock credit;
- reconnect should not silently lose a verified unlock after trial completion;
- telemetry records why a trigger failed during development;
- production UI should not leak hidden requirements before discovery.

# Xziel original challenge families inspired by the Relic concept

Without copying BO7's artifacts or fiction, original cursed modifiers could include:

- **Empty Mag** — reload discards the remaining magazine.
- **Cold Blood** — healing only begins after a kill.
- **Hollow Shops** — every purchase rotates the shop inventory.
- **No Echo** — minimap and hit markers disabled.
- **Debt Collector** — each down permanently raises prices for that match.
- **Hunter's Moon** — every fifth round is an elite hunt.
- **Glass Arsenal** — weapon rarity increases damage but also incoming damage.
- **Moving Ground** — standing still too long marks the player for a hazard.
- **Blood Pact** — co-op players share a percentage of incoming damage.
- **Famine** — normal ammo drops disabled; environmental kills restore ammo.

Unlock methods should be attached to map-specific fiction and never cloned one-for-one from Call of Duty.

# Key conclusion

The strongest idea to preserve is not any individual Relic.

It is this loop:

**discover a hidden mastery challenge → survive a specialized trial → permanently unlock a self-imposed disadvantage → combine disadvantages to access a higher challenge tier → use that higher tier to hunt even harder secrets**

That turns optional Easter Eggs into a long-term game mode rather than a checklist.
