# Call of Duty Zombies Main-Quest Compendium

Updated: 2026-09-20

This file is a **normalized quest-flow reference** for Xziel design work. It is intentionally written as a research blueprint, not as copied game text. Exact room names, randomized inputs, spawn variants and puzzle answers should be checked against the full walkthrough sources in `sources.md`.

Legend:
- **FULL** = complete Main Quest / Easter Egg chain.
- **OBJ** = objective-story structure rather than a classic hidden Main Quest.
- **SIDE** = no true full Main Quest; useful side-secret research.
- **SUPER** = cross-map completion chain.

---

# World at War (2008)

## Nacht der Untoten — SIDE
Useful lessons: pure survival readability, escalating pressure, mystery-box identity, almost no quest overhead.

## Verrückt — SIDE
Useful lessons: split-spawn co-op, power-gated reconnection, environmental storytelling, trap routes.

## Shi No Numa — SIDE
Useful lessons: randomized perk-machine huts, traversal risk, trap economy, hidden-song collectibles.

## Der Riese — SIDE
Research route:
1. Turn on power.
2. Link the teleporters.
3. Unlock Pack-a-Punch.
4. Trigger and complete the Fly Trap hidden-object sequence.
5. Study how a simple secret gives the map a second layer without dominating survival.

# Black Ops (2010)

## Kino der Toten — SIDE
Power → teleporter link → Pack-a-Punch. Side research: film reels, song collectibles, trap network, environmental clues.

## “Five” — SIDE
Power → DEFCON switches → Pack-a-Punch access. Side research: Pentagon Thief round, hidden song, high-pressure vertical routing.

## Ascension — FULL — Casimir Mechanism
Normalized route:
1. Turn on power and link the three Lunar Landers to open Pack-a-Punch.
2. Use a Gersch Device on the outside generator node.
3. Activate all four Space Monkey buttons together.
4. Hold the required pressure/clock step for roughly two minutes.
5. Ride the landers in the correct sequence to spell LUNA.
6. Complete the final Gersh-release weapon/item sequence.
7. Reward/payoff: Gersh is freed.

Design lesson: teach traversal first, then turn traversal devices into puzzle components.

## Call of the Dead — FULL
Normalized route:
1. Restore the map systems and acquire the quest-required Wonder Weapon/tools.
2. Communicate with the trapped original crew behind the sealed door.
3. Recover the fuse and restore their immediate systems.
4. Disable the security/power barriers required by the crew.
5. Find and solve the coded environmental clue chain.
6. Collect the Golden Rod / Vril device path objective.
7. Deliver the item to the crew and complete the final exchange.
8. Reward: story handoff plus Wonder Weapon reward.

Design lesson: NPC dialogue can provide strong diegetic quest direction without explicit HUD objectives.

## Shangri-La — FULL — Time Travel Will Tell
Normalized route:
1. Restore power and map traversal.
2. Trigger the eclipse/time-state mechanic.
3. Synchronize the early environmental interactions.
4. Use the pressure plates / symbols / water-slide and tunnel systems as puzzle gates.
5. Complete the Napalm Zombie and gas/valve interactions.
6. Solve the wall-symbol / tile / time-state sequence.
7. Acquire and charge the Focusing Stone.
8. Finish the final time-travel step and claim the reward.

Design lesson: a map-state transformation can make familiar spaces behave like a second map.

## Moon — FULL — Cryogenic Slumber Party / Big Bang Theory
Normalized route:
1. Reach Griffin Station, power the map and obtain the Hacker.
2. Complete Samantha Says.
3. Hack the required laboratory buttons.
4. Let Excavator Pi breach Tunnel 6, then disable it.
5. Move the black/Vril sphere through its interaction sequence.
6. Fill the MPD/soul containers and open Samantha’s chamber.
7. Retrieve the Casimir plates / Golden Rod prerequisites.
8. Charge the Golden Rod.
9. Perform the soul swap at the MPD.
10. Repeat Samantha Says at the missile terminal.
11. Arm and launch the missiles.
12. Finale: Earth is destroyed in the Big Bang Theory ending.

Design lesson: long quests feel coherent when every step serves one thematic machine or ritual.

# Black Ops II (2012)

## TranZit — FULL — Tower of Babble
Two mutually exclusive branches.

### Maxis route
1. Build the Power Station components, then turn power off for Maxis’ path.
2. Bring Turbines to the pylon.
3. Use the electric enemy (Avogadro) at the pylon under the required conditions.
4. Coordinate the turbine placements.
5. Complete the pylon empowerment for Maxis.

### Richtofen route
1. Keep power on.
2. Build the Jet Gun.
3. Use the Jet Gun at the pylon until it breaks/charges the step.
4. Acquire EMP grenades.
5. Coordinate the EMP/teleporter-lamp sequence.
6. Complete Richtofen’s pylon empowerment.

Design lesson: branch choice creates replay value and community debate with mostly shared geometry.

## Nuketown Zombies — SIDE
Research: randomized perk arrival, simple survival map with a major narrative reveal delivered through environment/audio rather than a quest chain.

## Die Rise — FULL — High Maintenance
1. Restore traversal systems and acquire/build the Sliquifier.
2. Activate the required elevator / symbol / floor interactions.
3. Coordinate the four-player standing/activation steps.
4. Follow either the Maxis or Richtofen branch.
5. Complete the branch-specific energy / zombie / Sliquifier interactions.
6. Finish the tower activation.

Design lesson: vertical traversal itself can be the puzzle, but player-count hard locks are frustrating and should be avoided in Xziel.

## Mob of the Dead — FULL — Pop Goes the Weasel
1. Use Afterlife to power local devices and collect the Warden’s Key.
2. Feed the three Cerberus heads to earn Hell’s Retriever.
3. Build the plane.
4. Collect the five blue skulls for the free Blundergat.
5. Build/upgrade the Acid Gat and obtain the Golden Spork path.
6. Fly to the Golden Gate Bridge and return; repeat the cycle three times.
7. Enter the prisoner-number sequence in Afterlife.
8. Collect/listen to the audio-tag progression.
9. Take the final flight.
10. Finale branch: Weasel survives or the mobsters kill Weasel.

Design lesson: make the quest reinforce the map’s fiction — the “cycle” is both story and gameplay loop.

## Buried — FULL — Mined Games
1. Open the town and use Arthur/Leroy to manipulate barriers and objects.
2. Build and place the required equipment.
3. Begin the Maxis or Richtofen branch.
4. Complete the branch’s crystal/orb/lantern/symbol setup.
5. Solve the sharpshooter / bell / target / energy sequences as required.
6. Synchronize the final team interactions.
7. Finish the side choice that resolves the Victis tower arc.

Design lesson: companion NPCs can become reusable world-interaction tools.

## Origins — FULL — Little Lost Girl
1. Power the six generators.
2. Build all four elemental staffs.
3. Upgrade all four staffs.
4. Place the staffs in the giant robots / excavation pedestals as required.
5. Obtain the One Inch Punch and upgrade it.
6. Use the Maxis Drone and special zombie drops for the purification steps.
7. Use the staffs/robot buttons to open the hidden route.
8. Kill the required enemies in the Crazy Place and complete the final soul charge.
9. Release Maxis / trigger the ending.

Design lesson: a “build four, upgrade four, use four” structure gives co-op teams clear parallel roles.

# Modern Warfare III Zombies / MWZ (2023)

## Urzikstan — OBJ / OPEN-WORLD STORY
MWZ is not a classic round-based single-map Easter Egg. Its useful design pattern is **persistent mission acts + extraction + seasonal Dark Aether quests**.

Research structure:
1. Deploy with a chosen mission.
2. Complete contracts and acquire gear/schematics.
3. Finish Act I mission chains and story mission.
4. Finish Act II mission chains and story mission.
5. Finish Act III mission chains and story mission.
6. Complete Act IV / Dark Aether story missions.
7. Discover hidden pedestal/item requirements for seasonal Dark Aether rifts.
8. Enter the rift, complete contracts/bosses, extract with persistent rewards.
9. Use schematics and stash progression to change future runs.

Design lesson: persistent meta-progression can make each match feed a larger quest campaign.

# Cross-game design observations

## Repeated step families
- Power / map opening.
- Pack-a-Punch unlock.
- Buildable or Wonder Weapon construction.
- “Soul box” / kill-in-zone charging.
- Environmental symbol transcription.
- Audio/light sequence repetition.
- Multi-location item scavenger hunt.
- Timed carry or escort.
- NPC dialogue handoff.
- Special-enemy manipulation.
- Use-a-specific-weapon interaction.
- Object transformation / upgrade.
- Randomized-per-match code.
- Team synchronization.
- Defense ritual / lockdown.
- Parkour/traversal test.
- Boss weak-point phases.
- Branching allegiance/ending.
- Persistent cross-map reward.

## Patterns Xziel should intentionally modernize
- Replace mandatory 4-player locks with solo-safe equivalents.
- Reduce blind RNG dependencies.
- Keep puzzle clues in-world but add strong success/failure feedback.
- Let players retry most setup puzzles without ending the run.
- Save “one attempt” tension for opt-in boss gates.
- Use map-specific mechanics in the boss.
- Add a quest journal that records *discovered clues*, not answers.
- Make quest logic network-authoritative so co-op state cannot desync.
- Build randomized puzzle seeds deterministically from the match seed.
