# Classic Exact Quest Orders — Black Ops 1 through Black Ops 4

Updated: 2026-09-20

Purpose: preserve dependency-accurate **quest skeletons** from the classic Treyarch era. These are original normalized research notes, not copied walkthrough prose. Exact randomized values and item spawn variants should always be checked against the current-match evidence and the links in sources.md.

The point is to record:
- what must happen before what;
- which mechanics recur;
- which steps are randomized;
- which historical player-count locks created friction;
- what the map teaches before its finale.

---

# BLACK OPS

## Ascension — Casimir Mechanism

### Required order
1. Turn on power.
2. Ride/link all three Lunar Landers and launch the rocket so Pack-a-Punch opens.
3. Throw a Gersch Device at the outside generator node.
4. During a Space Monkey round, have all four required buttons held/activated together.
5. Complete the pressure/clock stand for roughly two minutes.
6. Ride the Lunar Landers in the route that spells L-U-N-A.
7. At the final light/orb, use the required combination of Matryoshka Dolls, Gersch Device, upgraded Thundergun and Ray Gun.
8. Gersh is freed and the quest resolves.

### Historical friction worth recording
- Original BO1 requires four players.
- Several finale tools are Mystery Box dependent.
- An otherwise short quest can become long purely through box RNG.

### Xziel lesson
Team synchronization is fun; mandatory four-player population and unbounded required-item RNG are not.

## Call of the Dead — Stand-In / Ensemble Cast

### Shared start
1. Turn on ship power.
2. Knife/interact with the bunker door where the trapped Ultimis crew can be heard.
3. Find and install the fuse.

### Co-op expansion
4. Destroy the required generators around the map.
5. Recover Nikolai's vodka and deliver it through the bunker interaction.

### Golden Rod route
6. Obtain the V-R11.
7. Transform a zombie into a human.
8. Make the transformed human run into/through the lighthouse path.
9. Kill the transformed target under the required lighthouse conditions.
10. Recover the Golden Rod/Vril device.
11. Deliver it to the crew.
12. Finish the bunker/fuse interaction for the reward.

### Historical note
- Solo runs a different "Stand-In" version.
- Co-op adds steps rather than simply scaling health.

### Xziel lesson
Solo can have an **equivalent narrative route** instead of a crippled copy of the co-op route.

## Shangri-La — Time Travel Will Tell

### Required order
1. Restore underground power using the paired switches.
2. Enter Eclipse Mode using the spawn-area buttons.
3. Solve the paired stone-tile matching step.
4. Complete the water-slide pressure interaction.
5. Use the Baby Gun/shrink weapon on the required crystal/orb interaction.
6. Use a controlled Napalm Zombie to ignite the gas-leak path.
7. Seal the tunnel openings with mines/explosives.
8. Melee the sequence of wall symbols and complete the tent/dynamite interaction.
9. Solve the mud-room dial puzzle.
10. Upgrade the shrink ray to the Fractalizer.
11. Obtain the dynamite.
12. In the final eclipse, fire through the focusing-crystal network to shrink the meteor.
13. Raise the Pack-a-Punch route and use the dynamite.
14. Return to normal time and claim the Focusing Stone.

### Historical friction
- Original BO1 needs four players.
- Many stages happen only inside limited Eclipse windows.
- Failure often means re-entering Eclipse and repeating setup.

### Xziel lesson
A temporary alternate world state is excellent quest language. Player-count locks are not necessary to preserve it.

## Moon — Cryogenic Slumber Party / Big Bang Theory

### Part 1
1. Complete Samantha Says.
2. Hack the required laboratory/computer buttons.
3. Allow Excavator Pi to breach Tunnel 6.
4. Disable the excavator after the breach.
5. Move the Vril Sphere through its interaction sequence.
6. Fill the MPD tube/soul container.
7. Open Samantha's chamber.

### Part 2
8. Recover the Casimir plates from Area 51.
9. Charge the Golden Rod.
10. Perform the soul/body swap at the MPD.
11. Complete the three-round Samantha Says missile-arming sequence.
12. Pull/launch the missiles.
13. Use the final Gersch/Vril interaction to complete Big Bang Theory and destroy Earth.

### Historical gates
- Full BO1 completion depends on prior Call of the Dead/Shangri-La profile progression and the correct Richtofen inventory state.
- Hacker and P.E.S. compete for the same equipment slot.
- Excavator timing and Mystery Box items create major RNG.

### Xziel lesson
Cross-map continuity is exciting, but old save/profile prerequisites should be made visible and recoverable.

# BLACK OPS II

## TranZit — Tower of Babble

### Shared setup
1. Build the Turbine.
2. Reach the Power Station and assemble the power switch.
3. Open/understand the timed Town-bank Pack-a-Punch route.
4. Choose Maxis or Richtofen for the match.

### Maxis branch
5. Turn power back off.
6. Obtain EMP grenades.
7. Bring Turbines to the pylon.
8. Manipulate/kill Avogadro beneath the pylon under the correct powered state.
9. Place Turbines at the required street lamps.
10. Complete Maxis' tower state.

### Richtofen branch
5. Leave power on.
6. Build the Jet Gun.
7. Use it to heat/charge the pylon rock until the required state is reached.
8. Obtain EMP grenades.
9. Coordinate EMP hits on the four required street lamps/teleporter nodes.
10. Complete Richtofen's tower state.

### Xziel lesson
Branching can share most of the map and still produce radically different final mechanics.

## Die Rise — High Maintenance

### Required order
1. Turn on power.
2. Build the Trample Steam equipment.
3. Link/position the required elevators.
4. Activate the four floor symbols in the correct order.
5. Use sniper fire on the dragon-sign orbs.
6. Charge the resulting orbs.
7. Place the charged orbs on correctly positioned Trample Steams.
8. Place four Trample Steams on the zombie seals/markers.
9. Read the Mahjong-tile information to derive the tower order.
10. Use Galvaknuckles on the tower corners in the required sequence.
11. Complete the chosen Maxis or Richtofen ending state.

### Historical friction
- Original completion is four-player dependent.
- Vertical traversal can kill a run independently of quest execution.

### Xziel lesson
Verticality should create tactical decisions, but quest objects need safe retry paths after traversal failure.

## Mob of the Dead — Pop Goes the Weasel

### Required order
1. Use Afterlife to open/power the prison systems needed for setup.
2. Feed all three Cerberus heads.
3. Claim Hell's Retriever.
4. Collect the plane parts and build the plane.
5. Collect the five hidden blue skulls and claim the free Blundergat.
6. Complete the Golden Spork chain.
7. Fly to the Golden Gate Bridge.
8. Use Pack-a-Punch / Acid Gat progression while repeating the prison-to-bridge cycle.
9. Complete three flights/cycles.
10. In Afterlife, enter the prisoner-number sequence.
11. Follow the audio-tag/story progression.
12. Take the final flight without normal refueling.
13. At the bridge, resolve the branch: the mob kills Weasel, or Weasel kills the mob.

### Historical gate
- Most of the quest is solo-playable, but the ending itself needs at least two players and one must be Weasel.

### Xziel lesson
The strongest quests make the fiction and mechanics the same thing: repeated escape is literally the story's cycle.

## Buried — Mined Games

### Shared setup
1. Free Leroy/Arthur.
2. Turn on power.
3. Build the required equipment.
4. Choose/build either the Maxis Gallows or Richtofen Guillotine.
5. Break/charge the four map orbs.
6. Charge the lantern with souls.
7. Place the lantern at the Gunsmith roof.
8. Decode the sign clue and punch/activate the signs.
9. Spawn/guide the light or Wisp.
10. Use the Time Bomb at the required point.
11. Guide the light energy to the branch structure.
12. Trigger the fountain/wish stage.
13. Complete the synchronized sharpshooter target challenge.

### Historical gates
- Maxis and Richtofen have different minimum player counts.
- Some build parts are shared between the two branch structures; placing them on the wrong one locks the other branch for that run.

### Xziel lesson
Branch commitment should be explicit before consuming mutually exclusive resources.

## Origins — Little Lost Girl

### Required order
1. Activate all six generators.
2. Open the Crazy Place/Excavation staff chamber.
3. Build all four Elemental Staffs.
4. Upgrade all four into Ultimate Staffs.
5. Fill the four robot-footprint soul chests.
6. Every player claims the One Inch Punch.
7. Obtain the G-Strike for each required player.
8. Build the Maxis Drone.
9. **Ascend from Darkness:** place the staffs in the required robot/Excavation pedestals.
10. **Rain Fire:** press the robot button and throw the G-Strike at the seal.
11. **Unleash the Horde:** send Maxis Drone into the opened seal and kill the Panzer wave.
12. **Skewer the Winged Beast:** use Zombie Blood to find the invisible plane, shoot it, then find/kill the pilot.
13. **Wield a Fist of Iron:** each player punches the required glowing-arm Templars.
14. **Raise Hell:** return the staffs to the Crazy Place.
15. Complete the final soul charge.
16. Send the Drone/trigger the portal ending.

### Important gates
- All six generators need to have been active together after staff construction.
- Ice staff parts are weather-dependent.
- Giant robot timing shapes several steps.
- Unlike many BO1/BO2 quests, the full quest can be completed solo.

### Xziel lesson
Parallel weapon construction gives co-op teams natural jobs while still supporting solo mastery.

# BLACK OPS III

## Shadows of Evil — Apocalypse Averted

### Required order
1. Take the Summoning Key.
2. In each district, use Beast Mode to open the ritual route and collect the character's ritual item.
3. Complete all four rituals and receive the four Gateworms.
4. Place all Gateworms in the Sacred Place.
5. Complete the final ritual to activate Pack-a-Punch.
6. Ride the tram and read the three current-match sword symbols.
7. In Beast Mode, shock the matching symbols.
8. Claim the Apothicon Egg.
9. Charge it at the four Cthulhu statues with kills.
10. Claim the Apothicon Sword.
11. Complete each Arch-Ovum / Margwa ritual to upgrade the sword.
12. Interact with the book in Nero's room to start the endgame.
13. Carry the flag through the rift/Keeper defense route.
14. Complete the Keeper altar defenses.
15. Ready the Keepers in the Sacred Place.
16. Drop the Shadowman's shield and trap him in the Summoning Key.
17. For the full four-player ending, complete the post-Shadowman tram/Gateworm/Keeper synchronization.

### Historical friction
- Most of the quest works solo, but the full final ending historically requires four players.

### Xziel lesson
The map teaches rituals, Beast interactions, sword symbols and defense before asking the team to combine them.

## Revelations — For the Good of All

### Required order
1. Shoot the four gravestones in the correct sequence.
2. Activate all four Corruption Engines.
3. Stun/open the Apothicon so Pack-a-Punch can be reached.
4. Build the Keeper Protector.
5. Run the Keeper ritual.

7. Upgrade it to Estoom-Oth.
8. Shoot the hidden slabs/bone locations and assemble Sophia's body/bones.
9. Throw Lil' Arnies into the nine Apothicon holes and defeat the spawned Margwas.
10. Turn the Corruption Engines into turrets.
11. Summon/activate S.O.P.H.I.A.
12. Follow her to Kino and obtain the Kronorium.
13. Incubate the four Gateworm eggs.
14. Plant the completed eggs and collect the runes.
15. Enter the rune/challenge area and complete the trial sequence.
16. Enter the Shadowman encounter.
17. Use the Summoning Key mechanics to finish the confrontation.

### Meta note
When the required prior BO3 Main Quest state is present, Revelations acts as the Super Easter Egg culmination and can award persistent starting utility.

## Classified — endurance completion

### Progression
1. Turn on South Laboratories power.
2. Cycle the DEFCON switches and access the Panic Room.
3. Collect and build the Teleporter Signal Amplifier.
4. Install it on the War Room teleporter.
5. Set DEFCON 5 and use the center teleporter to reach Groom Lake/Pack-a-Punch.
6. Build the Riot Shield.
7. Charge the Project Skadi / Cold War Remedy window five times.
8. Recover the four aerial-photo codes.
9. Enter those codes at the War Room keypad in their fixed map order.
10. Survive three rounds at Groom Lake.
11. Claim Winter's Howl.
12. For the actual story ending, survive through round 149 so the round-150 cutscene triggers.

### Xziel lesson
Not every map needs a puzzle-boss finale. Survival endurance itself can be the mastery condition—but this should be clearly signposted if it gates story content.
