# Map-by-Map Optional Content Index

Updated: 2026-09-20

Purpose: track the optional systems, side Easter Eggs and repeat-run mechanics that are worth studying from each major Call of Duty Zombies map. This is an **engineering/design index**, not a verbatim walkthrough archive. Exact trigger locations remain linked in sources.md.

---

# World at War / Black Ops foundations

## Nacht der Untoten
Study:
- almost pure survival with virtually no quest overhead;
- Mystery Box as the central replay variable;
- environmental storytelling without explicit objectives.

Xziel lesson: one map in a game can deliberately be simple. Quest density should vary.

## Verrückt
Study:
- split-player spawn;
- power reunites the team;
- trap routing;
- strong environmental horror.

Xziel lesson: co-op separation can create tension without requiring a puzzle.

## Shi No Numa
Study:
- randomized perk-hut assignment;
- moving/trap route economy;
- early hidden-song collectible grammar.

## Der Riese
Study:
- teleporter linking;
- Pack-a-Punch as a map-wide setup ritual;
- Fly Trap secret;
- hidden dialogue/toy discovery with minimal material reward.

Xziel lesson: mystery itself can be the reward.

# Black Ops

## Ascension
Optional/system ideas:
- Lunar Landers serve traversal before becoming quest language.
- Space Monkey rounds attack purchased perks.
- hidden song/object interactions.

Xziel extraction:
- enemies can threaten the player's infrastructure/loadout rather than HP only.

## Call of the Dead
Optional/system ideas:
- George Romero is a persistent roaming boss rather than a single arena encounter.
- defeating him can pay a perk/Wunderwaffe-style reward.
- water changes movement/temperature pressure.
- scavenger-style weapon identity.

Xziel extraction:
- persistent roaming threat + map environment can form a second survival system.

## Shangri-La
Optional/system ideas:
- Eclipse Mode transforms the same geometry into a quest-state version.
- mine cart, water slide, geysers and moving walls are traversal toys.
- Focusing Stone completion can leave the collector with all perks for continued survival.
- classic hidden-song collection.

Xziel extraction:
- main-quest completion can transform the post-quest survival state instead of simply ending the match.

## Moon
Optional/system ideas:
- low gravity vs artificial gravity.
- Excavators threaten different tunnels.
- Hacker acts as a portable world-manipulation tool.
- Area 51 is a high-pressure no-round-number staging zone.
- completion permanently changes the sky/world state for that match and grants a major survival reward.
- multiple songs/hidden interactions.

Xziel extraction:
- environmental systems can be interactable tools and threats, not static scenery.

# Black Ops II

## TranZit
Study:
- moving bus as mobile safe-ish space and route scheduler.
- buildables distributed across map.
- teleport lamps and Denizen-assisted traversal.
- two mutually exclusive quest branches.

Xziel extraction:
- vehicle schedules create natural decisions: wait, run, or take a dangerous shortcut.

## Die Rise
Study:
- elevators move perks and Pack-a-Punch vertically.
- vertical fall risk shapes the whole map.
- buildable traversal devices.
- branch quest.

Xziel extraction:
- a moving utility is more memorable than another stationary machine.

## Mob of the Dead
Optional systems:
- Afterlife temporarily changes player verbs.
- Cerberus feeding unlocks Hell's Retriever.
- blue-skull hunt pays a free Blundergat.
- Golden Spork gives a long-form melee reward.
- multiple hidden songs.
- Brutus can disable perks, traps, Mystery Box and workbenches.

Xziel extraction:
- temporary alternate-form gameplay can reveal a second interaction layer.
- elites can sabotage infrastructure and force repair/recovery decisions.

## Buried
Optional systems:
- Leroy/Arthur can be directed with Candy or Booze.
- he can destroy barricades, manipulate objects and assist with map tasks.
- chalk weapon placements create a player-driven wall-buy layout.
- persistent bank/weapon-locker style economy in the BO2 era.
- free-perk and song secrets.

Xziel extraction:
- an NPC companion becomes much deeper when it is a **world tool**, not just AI firepower.

## Origins
Optional systems:
- Rituals of the Ancients challenge board pays staged rewards.
- Golden Shovel / Golden Helmet progression.
- Empty Perk Bottles can expand the perk limit.
- dig-site rarity/equipment economy.
- three hidden songs.
- giant-robot traversal and tank transport.

Xziel extraction:
- visible side challenges + hidden mastery progression can coexist.
- side quests can expand a system cap rather than simply hand out loot.

# Black Ops III

## Shadows of Evil
Documented optional-content density: very high.

Study:
- Beast Mode world-interaction layer.
- Civil Protector companion.
- Apothicon Servant construction.
- upgraded sword as a quest inside the Main Quest.
- trip mines/ritual interactions and hidden music.
- district-based objective architecture.

Xziel extraction:
- divide a large map into districts that each teach one variant of the same interaction grammar.

## The Giant
Study:
- classic teleporter/PAP structure.
- Fly Trap descendant.
- hidden Annihilator reward.
- snow/secret interactions and songs.

Xziel extraction:
- remixed legacy secrets reward players who recognize an earlier design language.

## Der Eisendrache
Documented optional-content density: extremely high.

Study:
- four elemental bow routes with distinct puzzle styles.
- low-gravity wall-run interactions.
- plunger/melee side reward.
- Panzer helmet.
- rocket timing.
- multiple songs and hidden interactions.

Xziel extraction:
- multiple weapon branches can share the same base tool while expressing different player skills.

## Zetsubou No Shima
Documented optional-content density: 11+ systems.

Standouts:
- **Imprint Plant:** stores a player's equipment/perk state and can restore it after death.
- **Golden Bucket:** permanent-in-match upgrade to the water-carry system.
- **Spider Bait:** temporarily play as a Spider.
- **Friendly Thrasher:** grow a plant mutation that creates an allied monster.
- plant breeding with multiple water types and outcomes.
- doppelganger scare.
- multiple hidden songs/free-perk systems.

Xziel extraction:
- crafting/gardening can have probabilistic outcomes while still giving players methods to influence the result.
- a side quest can literally modify respawn rules.

## Gorod Krovi
Documented optional-content density: 12+ systems.

Standouts:
- round/time milestones unlock progressively stronger melee weapons.
- Mangler/Valkyrie helmets reward enemy-specific mastery and grant narrow resistances.
- Dragon Wings reward traversal mastery.
- upgraded Monkey Bomb path.
- upgraded Dragon Strike.
- multiple music secrets and a free-perk challenge.

Xziel extraction:
- speedrun goals can live inside ordinary survival.
- cosmetics/wearables can also act as narrow in-match modifiers.

## Revelations
Documented optional-content density: 12+ systems.

Standouts:
- masks/hats tied to enemy kills or location challenges.
- free-perk wall-running challenge.
- time-trial rewards.
- upgraded/hidden weapons.
- cross-map Super Easter Egg reward structure.
- environment made from fragments of previous maps.

Xziel extraction:
- a finale map can remix mechanics from earlier maps while still hiding its own mastery layer.

- Rat King progression.

# Cross-map categories now represented in Xziel research

The research corpus now has concrete successful examples for:

- alternate-player-state gameplay;
- temporary transformation;
- companion NPC world manipulation;
- vehicle questing;
- moving utility machines;
- speedrun/time trials;
- challenge boards;
- hidden reward rooms;
- collectible hunts;
- song hunts;
- wearable modifiers;
- expandable perk/equipment caps;
- restore-on-death checkpoints;
- farming/gardening;
- minigames;
- arcade currency;
- traversal challenges;
- parkour;
- grapples;
- fishing;
- RC vehicles;
- claw machines;
- bartending/service games;
- delivery routes;
- randomized logic;
- astronomy/math/chemistry;
- murder/evidence deduction;
- world-state/timeline swaps;
- post-quest Aftermath states;
- branching finales;
- cross-map Super Quest progression;
- cursed risk/reward items;
- opt-in difficulty relics;
- persistent cosmetic challenge trees.

That breadth is the target for the **engine vocabulary**. Individual Xziel maps should use only the subset that fits their fiction and layout.
