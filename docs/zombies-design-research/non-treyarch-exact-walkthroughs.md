# Non-Treyarch Exact Quest Orders — Exo Zombies, Infinite Warfare & WWII

Updated: 2026-09-20

Purpose: preserve dependency-accurate quest structures from Sledgehammer and Infinity Ward Zombies. These games contain several systems Treyarch used less often: Exo-movement puzzles, secondary arcade economies, guest-character unlocks, persistent Director's Cut, guided/hardcore quest layers, secret-character feat trees and multi-map gauntlets.

---

# ADVANCED WARFARE — EXO ZOMBIES

## Outbreak — Game Over, Man!

### Core order
1. Restore the local room power needed to navigate the Atlas facility.
2. Acquire the Exo Suit so boost/dodge/slam interactions are available.
3. Recover the Black Box from the crashed Warbird.
4. Install the Black Box at the Morgue/story terminal.
5. Complete Lilith's character-card route using the EM1 laser interaction.
6. Complete Decker's character-card route in Exo Testing.
7. Complete Oz's card route through the Holding/Exo-Slam interaction.
8. Complete Kahn's card route using the map's medical/utility tools.
9. After all four named cards are owned, kill zombies/specials to collect dropped clearance cards.
10. Raise every active player's clearance to 49.
11. Each player uses the correct character reader to reach clearance 50.
12. Trigger the Atlas rescue extraction.

### Hidden mastery ending
A stricter variant uses no downs and melee-generated access cards to unlock the gold-statue alternate reward.

### Xziel lesson
Character-specific quest routes can encourage different exploration roles without changing the map geometry.

## Infection — MEAT IS MURDER

### Core order
1. Restore local power and obtain the Exo Suit.
2. Acquire the Magnetron from the 3D Printer; it is required for the cooking interactions.
3. Reach the spawn rooftop and obtain the golden frying pan.
4. Set all four orange sewer valves correctly to open the waterfall/blood-altar chamber.
5. Charge the pan at the altar.
6. Search the four districts for the raw meat pieces.
7. Cook each piece individually on the pan using the Magnetron until its completion cue.
8. Assemble the burger.
9. Manage any infection/decontamination pressure while transporting quest items.
10. Feed the completed burger to Bubby.
11. Recover the battery/tablet parts needed to reactivate him.
12. Restore Bubby.
13. Complete the Atlas rocket/launch sequence that resolves the quest.

### Xziel lesson
An intentionally mundane action—cooking a burger—becomes memorable when every ingredient requires a different risk or tool.

## Carrier — Flotsam & Jetsam

### Core order
1. Reconnect the split spawn through Cargo.
2. Acquire Teleport Grenades.
3. Open the Chompy route.
4. Power the hidden generator that activates the disposal systems.
5. Feed expendable weapons into the Weapon Disposal Machine until the three fishing-pole parts are obtained.
6. Build the fishing pole on the Gun Deck.
7. Complete the four tablet routes:
   - grenade-disposal sequence;
   - laser-maze route;
   - fishing/island/dig route;
   - shark-eye route.
8. Install all four tablets in the central vault.
9. Feed the Weapon Disposal Machine until C4 appears.
10. Take the C4.
11. Plant it on the vault/ship target.
12. Trigger the ship-sinking ending.

### Historical friction
Several required objects are RNG-fed by disposal/fishing. Losing a high-Mark weapon to the disposal machine is a punitive user error.

### Xziel lesson
If a quest consumes equipment, the UI must show **exactly what will be consumed** before confirmation.

## Descent — Reunion

### Core order
1. Turn all three Tidal Generator mechanisms until their blue-state confirmation appears.
2. Open the secret Tidal Generator room.
3. Repeatedly depressurize the Lounge airlock; progress persists between attempts.
4. Obtain an Exo Goliath suit when it begins dropping.
5. Enter the flooded route in the Goliath and activate the underwater switch.
6. Progress the first chain of Oz challenge rounds.
7. Enter the first Oz arena around the scripted round gate.
8. Defeat Oz's first form.
9. Complete the number-board challenge.
10. Complete the rocket/special-rule challenge.
11. Complete the memory/simulation challenge.
12. Continue the special round-rule sequence.
13. Reach the mutated Oz finale.
14. Defeat mutated Oz.
15. Unlock the harder Double Feature mode.

### Xziel lesson
A campaign finale can reward completion with a **new ruleset** rather than only an achievement.

# INFINITE WARFARE ZOMBIES

## Zombies in Spaceland

### Core order
1. Turn on all five local power switches.
2. Use all four park teleporters so the central Pack-a-Punch gateway opens.
3. Enter the projector room.
4. Release the four miniature UFOs.
5. Build at least one Weapon of Rock; a crafted Wonder Weapon is mandatory.
6. Collect the three SETI-COM parts.
7. Get the finished SETI-COM from David Hasselhoff.
8. Defend the device at its three designated sites, with increasing durations.
9. Return it and receive the four speakers.
10. Place the speakers around the Pack-a-Punch portal.
11. Record the speaker colors.
12. Reproduce the UFO's color sequence through three successful cycles.
13. Enter the Alien boss encounter.
14. Break its defenses and melee the back interaction to recover the two Alien Fuses.
15. Install the fuses.
16. Pack-a-Punch the Weapon of Rock.
17. Charge the first four final ring nodes.
18. Wait until the UFO is correctly aligned over the central portal.
19. Fire the fifth node to redirect the beam.
20. Claim the Soul Key.

### Xziel lesson
The theme-park attractions, arcade and speakers are ordinary map features first, quest devices second.

## Rave in the Redwoods — Locksmith

### Core order
1. Restore power in the Old Mine basement.
2. Collect the boat handle, engine and propeller.
3. Build the boat at the Docks.
4. Recover the two film reels.
5. Repair the Turtle Island projector.
6. Open Pack-a-Punch.
7. Speak with Kevin Smith.
8. Complete the first Rave ritual by dismembering the required arms, then kill its Slasher.
9. Complete the second ritual with the required leg removals, then kill its Slasher.
10. Complete the third ritual with headshots, then kill its Slasher.
11. Make final purchases before synchronizing the basement buttons; this changes the normal route.
12. Synchronize the required buttons (solo uses the solo-safe version).
13. Enter the boss arena.
14. Charge/complete the skull sites.
15. Use the Vlad/crossbow ritual mechanics against the Super Slasher.
16. Defeat him and take the Soul Key.

### Optional depth
- Charms are two-part mini-quests that grant persistent-in-match weapon/passive effects.
- Main Quest completion unlocks Kevin Smith as a selectable character.
- Rave Mode changes visibility and exposes secrets.

### Xziel lesson
A temporary visual/perception mode can be both exploration mechanic and identity.

## The Beast from Beyond

### Core order
1. Drop into the Ops Center and retrieve N31L's head.
2. Install it at Spawn to restore the first quest system.
3. Open the Theatre turret portal and the return portals.
4. Collect the three bridge scraps.
5. Repair the exterior bridge.
6. Open Pack-a-Punch.
7. Restore/advance N31L.
8. Obtain the Entangler.
9. Collect the four floppy disks.
10. Decode their current-match symbol/order information.
11. Insert them in the correct order.
12. Complete the sixteen-switch / grid-hacking chain.
13. Use the Entangler and map objects to finish the projector/film interactions.
14. Fully prepare for the Cryptid arena.
15. Enter the boss.
16. Survive Cryptid waves and complete the Beast damage cycles.
17. Defeat the final boss and take the Soul Key/Talisman reward.

### Meta progression
Completing all five IW maps unlocks Director's Cut. Replaying the campaign under Director's Cut creates the higher-tier Talisman loop that leads to the Mephistopheles super-boss.

### Xziel lesson
The strongest Super Quest reward starts a **new campaign layer** instead of ending progression.

# WWII NAZI ZOMBIES

## The Final Reich

### Core/hard quest order
1. Turn all three Village gas valves to their charged state.
2. Fire the Pilot Light trap to open the sewer floor.
3. Activate the main Sewer generator.
4. Reroute bunker power using the Morgue and Laboratory switches within their time window.
5. Open the Salt Mine/Emperor's Chamber route.
6. Activate the Pack-a-Punch tube network and ride all three disposal tubes, pressing the receiving buttons.
7. Charge the Geistkraft Device with kills.
8. Escort it through the required route.
9. Recover Tesla Gun barrel/core parts and build the Tesla Gun.
10. Complete the Right Hand of God fuse-color sequence.
11. Defend the Lightning Rod.
12. Recover the three Zeppelin batteries for the Left Hand of God.
13. Scan the four Raven paintings with the Brenner-head clue tool.
14. Convert/read the current-match Roman-numeral information.
15. Enter the Voice of God sequence.
16. Start the Panzermörder/Klaus fight.
17. Use charged batteries to stun the boss through three damage cycles.
18. Recover the artifact and complete the Fireworks ending.

### Key design feature
The game exposes a Guided/Casual quest layer with objective markers while hiding a deeper Hardcore completion route.

### Xziel lesson
Guided Mode and Discovery Mode should use the same underlying quest graph with different clue presentation.

9. Collect the three Son-of-Nerthus statues.
10. Extract the anointed spines from the Wüstling, Meuchler and Pest enemy types.
11. Build/repair the radio.
12. Use it to sink the warships.
13. Place the four heads in the ritual chamber.

## The Tortured Path — I Have the Power

### Global structure
This is three consecutive maps/chapters. Each stage has objective waves and its own Pack-a-Punch battery schedule. Cross-chapter success matters.

### Into the Storm
1. Charge the three stage batteries on their designated waves.
2. Collect six zombie body parts.
3. Obtain the two ropes and two rods.
4. Manipulate two Wüstlings so one kills the other and drops the arm-club.
5. Use the club to jam the windmill.
6. Assemble the corpse on the blade.
7. Lightning-charge it.
8. Escort the revived zombie.
9. Continue charging it until the carried battery is ready.
10. Install the battery and obtain the Hilt.
11. Defeat the Raketenbrenner using the airstrike shield-break mechanic.
12. Extract to chapter 2.

### Across the Depths
13. Enter Barracks electrical vision.
14. Shoot all nine spectral fish.
15. Charge all three Red Herring stations.
16. Clear the three-map island gauntlet.
17. Win the three-round cup/fish tracking game.
18. Obtain the Pommel.
19. Defeat the Stadtjäger.
20. Extract to chapter 3.

### Beneath the Ice
21. Break the vision battery and read the current-match rune information.
22. Enter the first rune-door code.
23. Place/forge the Hilt.
24. Throw flares into the four Forge bowls and the fifth ground bowl.
25. Recover/place the next Rune.
26. Solve the second rune sequence.
27. Complete the four sacrifice pads.
28. Recover the final Rune.
29. Solve the third code.
30. Place the Blade and assemble the Sword of Barbarossa.
31. Fight the Guardian.
32. During healing phases, destroy the lit overhead pillars.
33. Finish the Guardian and extract.

### Xziel lesson
A Zombies "campaign" can be several compact maps with carried meta-state rather than one enormous level.

## The Frozen Dawn — Kingfall

### Core order
1. Open Thule using the spawn/kill gate.
2. Use the three Lost in Blood transport trips to raise Pack-a-Punch and open fast travel.
3. Obtain the Flail.
4. Complete its trials and transform it into the Talon of Lu'roth.
5. Obtain the Hammer.
6. Complete its trials and transform it into the Fist of Tal'rek.
7. Obtain the Scythe.
8. Complete its trials and transform it into the Fang of An'heist.
9. Obtain the Shield.
10. Complete its trials and transform it into the Roar of Sang'ket.
11. Place all four upgraded weapons in the Ice Caves ritual positions.
12. Open the Morgue chamber.
13. Reclaim the weapons.
14. Take the final Thulian Transport—the point of no return.
15. Enter the God-King arena.
16. Use the four weapon mechanics through the boss phases.
17. Defeat the God-King and finish Kingfall.

### Xziel lesson
One weapon-per-player co-op roles can still remain solo-capable if the player can cycle the weapons instead of needing simultaneous owners.
